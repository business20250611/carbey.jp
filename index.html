Carbeyとは — About Carbey: Japan's Used Car Franchise Platform

カーベイ株式会社（Carbey Inc.）は、AIデータ分析と自動売買システムを核とした中古車フランチャイズサービスを提供しています。 Carbey Inc. provides a used car franchise service built around AI-powered data analysis and an automated trading system. Our platform makes it possible for anyone — regardless of experience, capital, or location — to start and operate a profitable used car dealership business in Japan. 中古車業界の参入障壁をテクノロジーで解消し、誰でも安心して始められる中古車ビジネスの仕組みを提供します。

The Japanese used car market circulates millions of vehicles every year, yet entry has traditionally required deep expertise, significant capital, and a physical storefront. Carbey changes all of that with our proprietary automated trading platform, franchise support network, and the E-Flow working capital solution.

事業内容 — Our Services: AI-Powered Used Car Trading System & Franchise

カーベイの中核サービスは、AIと独自データベースを組み合わせた中古車自動売買システムです。 The core of the Carbey service is our AI-driven used car automated trading system. It performs real-time market analysis to automatically calculate optimal purchase and sale prices. Franchise members simply follow the system's guidance — no specialist knowledge required.

フランチャイズモデルの最大の特徴は、仕入れ・整備・販売のすべての工程をサポートするワンストップ体制です。 Our franchise model provides end-to-end support covering vehicle sourcing, reconditioning, and sales through online auctions and our proprietary sales channels. No storefront, no large inventory, no prior industry experience needed. Run the business from home, part-time, or as a full-time independent dealership.

E-Flow — Working Capital Finance for Used Car Inventory

E-Flowはカーベイが提供する中古車仕入れ向け資金調達支援サービスです。 E-Flow is Carbey's financing support service designed specifically for used car inventory procurement. Traditional bank loans are too slow and too rigid for the fast-moving used car market. E-Flow provides fast, flexible working capital so franchise members can seize inventory opportunities without cash-flow constraints. 資金面での参入障壁を取り除き、より多くの方が中古車ビジネスに挑戦できる環境を整えています。

Learn more about our financing options on the E-Flow service page.

私たちのビジョン — Our Vision: Democratizing the Car Dealership Business

「儲かる車屋をみんなの手に」— Our mission is to make profitable car dealerships accessible to everyone. We believe that with the right technology and support system, anyone who wants to start a car business should be able to do so. Carbey removes the five traditional barriers to entry:

  1. Complex market research and pricing know-how (相場リサーチの複雑さ)
  2. Vehicle reconditioning skills and cost management (板金塗装・整備コスト)
  3. Dependency on a storefront and staff (店舗・人員の必要性)
  4. High and rising operating costs in modern business (販管費の高騰)
  5. Cash-flow risk and working capital shortage (資金繰りリスク)

By solving all five with technology, data, and a proven support framework, Carbey opens the door for part-time entrepreneurs, salaried employees seeking side income, and full-time independent operators alike.

よくある質問 — Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Can I join the Carbey franchise with no prior experience in the car industry?
A. Yes. Our automated trading system and data tools handle the expertise side. You need a second-hand dealer license (古物商許可証) and basic computer skills — that is all.
Q. Do I need a storefront or a physical lot?
A. No storefront required. All buying and selling is done through online auctions and digital sales channels, so you can operate from home.
Q. How quickly can E-Flow provide financing?
A. Funds can typically be arranged within a few business days of application. Contact us for details specific to your situation.

会社概要 — Company Overview

カーベイ株式会社（Carbey Inc.）was established in June 2025 and is headquartered in Atsugi City, Kanagawa Prefecture, Japan. We are a technology company driving digital transformation (DX) in the automotive industry and working toward the democratization of used car trading. For full company details visit our company information page.

For reference on the Japanese used car industry, see the Japan Used Car Dealers Association (日本中古自動車販売協会連合会). Industry data from Japan Automobile Dealers Association (日本自動車販売協会連合会) is also a useful resource.
